# Current-resource transfer and live life steal (schema 7 / mechanism 105 / protocol 6)

A strict hostile `unit_effect` may declare `resource_steal`. No new client input exists: cast by exact target identity through `cast_unit`. Native hit triggers may use `steal_other` with `direct_hit` or `basic_hit` (the latter excludes skills). Configuration is loaded and executed by Rust. Node transports private results without calculating transfers.

```json
{"resource_steal":{"hp_current_bps":1000,"spirit_current_bps":1000,"hp_policy":"direct_transfer"}}
```

Both ratios are required, range 0–10000, with at least one positive. `hp_policy` is required exactly when the HP ratio is positive. Accepted strings are `direct_transfer` and `damage`; explicit null, unknown fields, an object in place of a string, and HP policy on SP-only effects are rejected. HP requires configured combat rules. A channel uses its existing total distribution and final remainder policy. Instant casts freeze immediately before committed effects; channels freeze once before activation effects; triggers freeze at their native fact time. Budgets use target **current** resources and are never recomputed each pulse.

Effect order is primary unit damage, resource transfer, then configured successful-pulse status gates. A legal zero-result pulse still advances; an invalid pulse does not. SP is removed before HP can trigger death cleanup. Each requested share is limited by current availability. Source SP return obeys live cap, deficit, and spirit-recovery lock. HP return uses actual HP removed, source healing-received multiplier, live cap and deficit. No HP is returned to a dead/invalid source. Overflow is reported and discarded. Interruption, death, generation loss, revocation, reconnect and close cancel future shares without rolling back earlier effects or granting them again.

`direct_transfer` bypasses defense, tier amplification, damage reduction and shields, while preserving damage immunity, death guards, kill ownership and cleanup. It emits no ordinary Damage/DirectHit/DamageDealt/DamageTaken, damage-window record, reflection, or life-steal side effect. `damage` uses the shared physical damage pipeline, including applicable tier, defense, reduction, shield, damage facts and reflection. It does not roll an extra critical/dodge check or synthesize another DirectHit. Actual HP removed can exceed the requested raw HP share through amplification. A reflected death is authoritative before resource healing and before any future channel pulse.

`resource_stolen` is delivered only to involved member source/target, with optional action identity and explicit HP policy, requested/removed HP and SP, HP multiplier and adjusted return, actual restored HP/SP, and discarded excess. A successful HP removal also has `heal_resolved` with `heal_kind: "resource_steal"`; no HP removal produces no artificial healing result. NPC owners use the same pipeline and target-member privacy. Private channel budgets are never published to other members. Financial recovery remains compensation-only; it never resumes or replays battle transfers.

A timed scalar or compound numeric status can add `life_steal` in basis points, including signed reductions, source-scoped stacks and existing cleanup rules. On direct damage, the live status aggregate is added to the attack's static life-steal rate; clamp to 0 and `combat_rules.life_steal_cap_bps` (default 10000, configurable 0–1000000), multiply actual HP damage, then floor once. Periodic/counter damage retains only its explicit existing static rule; independent resource transfer never applies life steal a second time. Healing uses the same received-healing and cap gates. The ordinary status projection carries definition, layers, source and deadline; the client displays it without calculating a return.

`steal-a.json` / `steal-b.json` and their four/eight-member accounts exercise independent/damage policies, Nth-pulse full life-steal status, exact final remainder, cancel/reconnect and source exit. They are declared test configurations. Original source 10%/three-pulse and actual 吞元 status evidence is in `src/verification/source_resource_steal_vectors.json`; original HP-policy choice is pending in `docs/decisions/SDK-5-88-resource-steal-policy.md`. Cultivation stages and full derived source power remain open. These samples do not assert acceptance of the complete original skill.
